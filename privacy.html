Privacy Policy — ProTech Invoice Poster

Effective date: October 6, 2026
Provider: ProTech Consulting and Engineering, Inc., 1208 Main Street, Redwood City, CA 94063 (“ProTech,” “we”)

1. Scope

This policy covers the private internal app “ProTech Invoice Poster” (“the App”), which creates invoices in ProTech’s QuickBooks Online company from ProTech office invoice PDFs. The App is not a consumer product and is not publicly distributed.

2. Data we process

Depending on how the App is used, we may process:

  • Invoice details from ProTech PDFs (invoice number, date, amounts, line items, job/site identifiers)
  • Customer bill-to and ship-to names and addresses as they appear on those invoices
  • QuickBooks Online identifiers needed to create or match customers, items, and invoices
  • OAuth tokens that authorize the App to access ProTech’s QuickBooks Online company

We do not intentionally collect Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, or bank login credentials through the App.

3. How we use data

We use this data only to create and manage invoices in ProTech’s own QuickBooks Online company and to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App. We do not sell personal information. We do not share Intuit customer data with third parties for their marketing.

4. Intuit / QuickBooks

When connected, the App exchanges data with Intuit under Intuit’s terms and ProTech’s QuickBooks Online settings. Intuit’s own privacy practices apply to data Intuit holds.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and operational logs are stored with restricted access for ProTech’s authorized systems and personnel. We use HTTPS for API calls. Tokens can be revoked in QuickBooks connected apps and rotated in the Intuit Developer portal.

6. Retention

Invoice-related records follow ProTech’s normal business and QuickBooks retention practices. App connection tokens are kept only while the App remains authorized.

7. Your choices

Authorized ProTech users can stop using the App and can ask ProTech to disconnect it from QuickBooks Online. For privacy questions or requests related to this App, contact glen@protech-cal.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
